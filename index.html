      Keyboard shortcuts

      Shortcuts work when the cockpit itself has focus — click any pane header or the top bar first. Once you click inside an app, that app has the keyboard.

      Alt1–4 focus a pane
      AltM maximize the focused pane / restore
      Esc restore from maximized
      AltR refresh the focused pane
      AltH hide or show the top bar
      Alt= / Alt- / Alt0 zoom the focused pane in / out / reset
